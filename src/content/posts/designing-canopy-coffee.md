CanopyCoffee began as a complete cafe design exercise in Revit for VLK Architecture. The project moved from an empty brief through space planning, construction documents, and a rendered proposal presented as one coherent architectural idea.

## Planning the room

The first problem was not the final image but the sequence of use: how a guest enters, reads the counter, waits, sits, and moves through the space without crossing the working paths behind the bar. The plan became the shared foundation for every later decision.

## Drawing and modeling together

Working in Revit kept the visual proposal connected to its construction logic. Plans and three-dimensional views were not separate deliverables; changes to proportion, circulation, or the counter had to remain believable in both.

## Presenting a complete idea

The final project was presented to a panel of professional architects. That constraint encouraged a direct story: state the concept, show how the plan supports it, and use the rendering to communicate atmosphere rather than hide unresolved spatial decisions.
